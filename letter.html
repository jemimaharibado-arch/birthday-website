October 20, 2026

To my baby,

Happy 18th birthday. 🤍

I’ve been sitting here for a while trying to figure out what to write in this letter, but honestly, simple words don't seem enough to explain how much you truly mean to me. Turning 18 is such a huge milestone, and seeing you step into adulthood today makes me so overwhelmingly proud of the person you’ve become. Looking back at how far you've come, I just feel so lucky to have shared so many memories with you along the way.

Habang pinapanood kitang lumaki at maging mas mature at independent, mas lalo akong nagiging proud sa’yo. You’ve grown into such a strong, genuine, and amazing person inside and out, and anyone who knows you can see how special you are. I know you're more than ready to take on the world and do great things, but no matter how old we get or how busy life becomes, you will always be that special person who holds a really big, permanent place in my heart.

Gusto ko ring gamitin 'tong chance na 'to para mag-sorry sa lahat ng mga naging pagkakamali ko sa’yo. I am really, truly sorry for the times I upset you, said the wrong things without thinking, or fell short of being the kind of person you deserve. It really breaks my heart whenever I realize I've caused you stress or pain, because your happiness and peace of mind honestly matter so much to me, and I never want to be the reason for your tears.

Thank you so much for being so patient with me, even on the days when I was hard to deal with, and for always understanding my flaws. Maraming-maraming salamat kasi kahit minsan sobrang gulo ko o mahirap ako intindihin, hindi mo pa rin ako sinukuan at pinili mo pa ring magtiwala sa akin. Your kindness and understanding mean the absolute world to me, and I promise you that I’m constantly working on being a better person for you every single day.

Sobrang swerte ko talaga na dumating ka sa buhay ko, and I thank God for your presence every day. You bring so much light, comfort, and good energy into my life, and your laugh is honestly still my favorite sound in the whole world. Hindi ko na ma-imagine kung ano ang magiging buhay ko kung wala ka, and I’m just so incredibly thankful na nandito ka at magkasama pa rin tayo up until now.

As you officially start this brand new chapter of adulthood, I hope you go after all your big dreams without ever doubting yourself for even a second. The world is huge and can feel overwhelming at times, but don't ever forget how smart, capable, resilient, and beautiful you are. Kayanin mo lahat ng pagsubok kasi alam na alam kong kayang-kaya mo. I can't promise that I'll know exactly what every chapter ahead will look like, but I can keep choosing to show up, listen, understand, and support you the best way I can.

Gusto kong malaman mo na kahit anong mangyari at kahit saan ka man dalhin ng buhay, nandito lang ako lagi sa tabi mo. I will always be here to listen to your rants, celebrate your wins, and cheer you on through every high and low. Kahit magbago ang mga bagay-bagay sa paligid natin habang tumatanda tayo, hindi kailanman magbabago 'yung pagpapahalaga ko sa'yo. So, happy 18th birthday, my baby. I hope this year brings you new memories, reasons to smile, opportunities to grow, and reminders of how loved and appreciated you are.

Sana ngayong 18th birthday mo, maramdaman mo talaga kung gaano ka kahalaga at kamahal ng lahat ng tao sa paligid mo, lalo na ako. You deserve all the good things, peace, and happiness in the world today and for all the years ahead. Enjoy your debut to the absolute fullest and make amazing memories. And if there's one thing I hope you take away from this letter, it's that I'm really, really glad our paths crossed.

Someone who's really glad it's you. 🤍
